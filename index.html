How it works

Three steps. Zero tab-switching.

Pick your pool

Choose the champions you are comfortable with for each role. The app only ever recommends from this list.

Enter champion select

Draft Coach detects the lobby on its own, reads the enemy picks and ranks your pool against them.

Lock in

When you lock in, runes, items and summoner spells are imported into the client for you.

Draft from your phone

Queue up, step away from the desk and keep control of your champion select. Scan a QR code in the desktop app and your phone becomes a remote for your own League client.

  • Accept or decline the match when the queue pops.
  • See the recommendations, hover and lock in your champion, and ban.
  • Pick your runes and summoner spells before the game starts.
  • Paired with your PC through a one-time QR code. Revoke the phone from the desktop app at any time.

Every action is a tap you make yourself: the phone only relays your choice to your own client, during champion select. Nothing is automated and nothing runs during the match. This feature will only ship once it is approved by Riot Games.

The data

Built from high-elo matches

Every number in the app comes from real games, and every number shows how many games are behind it.

  • Ranked solo queue matches of Master+ players, plus the specialists of each champion.
  • Aggregated on our server every patch through the official Riot Games API.
  • Early in a patch, the previous patch is blended in until there is enough sample.
  • Win rates always come with the sample size, and small samples are flagged.

Fair play & privacy

Built to play by the rules

Draft Coach is a champion select tool. It stays out of the match and out of your way.

No in-game overlay

Nothing is drawn on top of the match. The app lives in its own window and is meant for the draft.

No tracking during the game

No timers and no tracking of enemy cooldowns. The cooldown table is static game data you read before the match.

Never targets other players

It never reveals or ranks other players. Recommendations are about champions and matchups only.

Game files untouched

The app does not modify game files. It only imports runes, items and spell sets through the client.

Official endpoints only

Only the official Riot Games API and League Client endpoints are used. No API key is shipped inside the app.

Your history stays with you

Your match history stays on your PC. It is used locally to personalize recommendations and never uploaded.

FAQ

Questions

Is it allowed?

Draft Coach uses only the official Riot Games API and the League Client, and follows Riot's developer policies. Product registration with Riot Games is pending.

Does it play for me?

No. It recommends, you decide. Every pick, ban and lock-in is made by you.

Which roles are supported?

All five roles, starting with top, jungle and ADC.

Is it free?

Yes. There will always be a free tier.

Does it work on Mac?

Windows only for now.
